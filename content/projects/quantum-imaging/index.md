title: Quantum Imaging & Microscopy
summary: Entangled-photon microscopy and quantum super-resolution imaging beyond the classical diffraction limit.
date: 2023-07-01
tags:
  - Quantum Imaging
---

We develop quantum imaging techniques that use entangled photons to beat classical resolution and noise limits. Highlights include quantum microscopy of biological cells at the Heisenberg limit (*Nature Communications* 2023), quantum imaging of biological organisms through spatial and polarization entanglement (*Science Advances* 2024), above-twofold quantum super-resolution microscopy with entangled biphotons (*Science Advances* 2026), and a review & perspective on quantum super-resolution imaging (*Nanophotonics* 2025, cover article).
