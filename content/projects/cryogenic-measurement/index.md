We develop precision measurement tools for cryogenic environments, including nanofilm thermal-strain optomechanical thermometry (*Photonics Research* 2026, cover article) and time-correlated single-photon counting readout of optomechanical thermal effects (*Applied Physics Letters* 2025). We also study interfacial heat transport in quantum-device materials and vortex dynamics in superfluid helium.
