---
title: Near-Field Nano-Imaging (TERS)
summary: Tip-enhanced Raman spectroscopy and imaging with single-molecule, single-base resolution.
date: 2023-07-01
tags:
  - TERS
  - Plasmonics
---

We push tip-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (TERS) toward chemical imaging at the ultimate spatial limit. We achieved the first near-field Raman imaging of single-stranded DNA with single-base resolution (*JACS* 2018) and resolved the sequence of RNA strands at room temperature (*ACS Photonics* 2020, cover article). Current directions include nanocavity plasmon-mode imaging, tip-enhanced photoluminescence of 2D materials, and TERS of biological systems such as fungal cell walls.
